# CHANGELOG

1. Saved searches. Set up a search, hit Save and give it a name, and Load brings the whole thing
back later: the lobby, the characters, the ascension and every criterion. Handy for the searches
you run again and again, like two players as Ironclad and Silent looking for Silken Tress.

2. Your presets are kept outside the app folder, so installing a new build of the seed finder
leaves them alone. The Save and Load windows both show you exactly where they are, with a button
to copy the path.

3. Massive Scroll and Scroll Boxes can now be searched on, the same way Arcane Scroll and Hefty
Tablet already could. Pick the relic in the Neow panel, then name the cards you want it to hand
over.

4. Scroll Boxes knows about bundles. It offers you two bundles of three and you keep a whole
bundle, so cards you ask for have to turn up in the same one. Results show the two bundles
separately instead of as a list of six.

5. Loading a preset saved on an older game version still works. Anything a patch has retired is
dropped and the tool tells you how much, instead of quietly loading a weaker search.
