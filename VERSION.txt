# CHANGELOG

1. Small Capsule, Large Capsule and Neow's Bones can now be searched on. Pick one in the Neow
panel, then name the relics you want it to hand over, and for Neow's Bones the curse too. Every
result also shows what these options would give each player.

2. Lead Paperweight can now be searched on. Pick it in the Neow panel, then name the colourless
cards you want it to offer.

3. Neow's Bones can now be set under "took at Neow". Fight card rewards are then read from where
that player's stream really starts on each seed, since what Neow's Bones costs depends on the two
relics it deals.

4. The shoutout and the fan-project notice now sit in their own band at the bottom of the page,
reached by scrolling down, instead of always taking up space on screen.

# FIXES

1. Massive Scroll searches and results now use your ascension. From Ascension 7 its rare odds are
lower, and the tool was using the Ascension 0 odds, so some cards it showed were wrong.

2. Map Visual now draws the map for the ascension and lobby a result was found with. Changing
either setting after a search no longer changes the map of a result already on screen.
