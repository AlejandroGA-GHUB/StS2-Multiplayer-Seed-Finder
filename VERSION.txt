Release Date: 11/12/2026

v0.113.0 beta steam release

This version predicts v0.113.0 only. The patch changed how the game shuffles, so seeds found
with it will not match on v0.111.0, and v1.8.0 does not match on v0.113.0.

# CHANGELOG

1. The Fool can now be picked as a character, with its own cards and relics in every search,
and as a Character Cards tickbox in Custom runs.

2. The new events can be searched on: Waterfall of Obvious Secrets and Wuppy in Trouble in the
Underdocks, Shrine of the Warlord and Thermae Nonupeipe in Glory, and Museum of Torture in Act 2.

3. The curse Neow's Bones adds is now exact for every player in co-op, so it can always be
searched on. It can also be combined with any relic Neow's Bones deals, including Kaleidoscope
and Phial Holster.

# FIXES

1. Bosses, events, Ancients, shop relics, chests, card rewards, Neow's offer and the act maps
all match v0.113.0 again.

2. When your game updates, the warning banner no longer claims Neow's offer and the Act 1 map are
still correct, since a patch like this one can change them too.

3. Checking a run played with a modded character now says so, instead of crashing.
