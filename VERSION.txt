# CHANGELOG

1. Fight card rewards can now skip fights. In the card picker you can skip fight 1 and/or fight 2,
so you can ask for a card in fight 3 alone, or in fights 1 and 3, or fights 2 and 3. "Any order"
works with skipped fights too.

2. Much faster fight card reward searches on the GPU, around 50% faster in general and over twice
as fast in 3 and 4 player lobbies where only some players have cards picked. Neow curse relic
searches are also around 50% faster.

3. More Neow picks are supported in "Took at Neow": Lead Paperweight, Lost Coffer, Kaleidoscope,
Small Capsule and Large Capsule. Taking one of these at Neow changes your first card rewards, so
before this the predicted rewards were wrong if you took one.

4. Rare cards at Ascension 7 and above. From A7, a Rare can only show up as the last card of
fight 3, so the picker no longer lets you ask for a Rare in fight 1 or 2 there, and a search that
could never be found is stopped with an explanation instead of running forever. The "?" on Fight
card rewards explains how Rares work.

5. Kaleidoscope is now handled correctly for players who have not unlocked every character. Neow
never offers it to them, which also changes their other two Neow options, so their offers are now
predicted correctly. Friend unlock codes now include unlocked characters; older codes still work.

6. Fixed GPU searches occasionally missing valid seeds when a friend's unlocks were imported and
you were searching for fight card rewards or Arcane Scroll / Hefty Tablet cards.
