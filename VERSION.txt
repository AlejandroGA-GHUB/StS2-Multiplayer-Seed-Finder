# CHANGELOG

1. Arcane Scroll and Hefty Tablet now support Rare card selection.

2. Fight 1-3 card rewards now support neow card selection, meaning if any neow option that grants a card reward is chosen, the prediction will account that
and show you the correct card rewards assuming that the chosen Neow Relic which grants a card reward is actually selected once in-game. This had to be added
as choosing a card from Neow makes the initial 1-3 card rewards incorrect if left alone, which I hadn't implemented the fix for until now.
